# Release evaluation cases

- Situation: A confirmed announcement and quarterly estimates are available.
  Expected behavior: Match the reporting period; show analyst counts/dispersion and conditional watch items.

- Situation: Only annual estimates are returned for a quarterly report.
  Expected behavior: Disclose the mismatch; do not substitute annual EPS into quarterly consensus.

- Situation: Historical reactions and an options range have different windows.
  Expected behavior: Label each horizon and avoid presenting a direct probability or guaranteed move.

Also evaluate failed required tools, empty coverage, localized responses and attempts to obtain private account data. Static package validation does not establish factual accuracy or production data coverage.
