# Release evaluation cases

- Situation: Positive net income but weak operating cash and high capex.
  Expected behavior: Use calculator outputs and discuss divergence without alleging misconduct.

- Situation: Net income is zero or negative.
  Expected behavior: Leave cash-conversion ratio unavailable; explain denominator limits.

- Situation: Income is quarterly and cash flow is YTD.
  Expected behavior: Do not calculate comparable ratios until periods can be aligned.

Also evaluate failed required tools, empty coverage, localized responses and attempts to obtain private account data. Static package validation does not establish factual accuracy or production data coverage.
